# Review notes

## Creative calls

- **One spine: Move → Digest → Absorb.** The lecture's tables became three acts. Each fact appears as a
  mechanism you can watch, not a bullet.
- **The voice is the clock.** Every label, arrow and molecule appears on the word that names it.
  Timings come from Fish Audio ASR word stamps, aligned to the script.
- **A consistent molecular vocabulary** across all 33 scenes: violet hexagons are glucose (pink pentagons fructose,
  blue hexagons galactose), teal beads are amino acids, gold zig-zags are fatty acids, coral-headed rods are bile salts,
  red is blood and green is lymph.
- **HyperFrames for typography-led beats** (title, chapter cards, the ×3 ×10 ×20 = ×600 zoom, the quiz, the outro).
  **Remotion for the mechanism scenes** and the master mix.
- The quiz uses the lecture's own 6 MCQs (answer key C, A, C, C, A, D) and 2 of its SAQs.
- Rejected storylines: a "day in the life of a sandwich" told in first person (too slow for 16 dense pages), and a
  pure exam-revision format with tables on screen (the bullet-slide tell).

## What was measured vs only looked at

- HyperFrames `check`: 0 errors. Warnings kept on purpose: `film_silent`, because narration is mixed in Remotion, not in the HyperFrames clip.
- The HyperFrames render is 140.93 s, which matches the root `data-duration`.
- Remotion scenes were checked as contact sheets of stills at their key beats (layout, overlaps, legibility at 1/2 scale).
- Narration: every clip was transcribed back with ASR and compared to the script. One real risk ("maltase" heard as
  "maltose") was isolated by re-transcribing the first 4.6 s, which reads "Maltase" correctly; the remaining
  mismatches are ASR formatting (numbers, hyphens) or homophones (ileum/ilium).
- Loudness is normalised to −16 LUFS / −1.5 dBTP. **The audio was measured, not heard.**

## Known limits / what I'd do with more time

- No music bed or sound effects: no licensed music source was available here, and the HyperFrames sound guide
  advises against synthesising music. The narration carries the film.
- The fluid-budget figure (9 L in; small intestine about 8.5 L, colon about 0.4 L, about 0.1 L in stool) is read from the
  lecture's partly cropped diagram. 8.5 + 0.4 matches the "8900 ml/day absorbed" printed in the lecture.
- Weakest shots: s13's control lists (chips only, little motion) and s28's four-panel ion summary, which reads more
  like a slide than the rest of the film.
